In plain language: Wallace Promo Posts is for personal business use. It connects only to the Pinterest account that Wallace Briggs authorizes, solely to manage and publish his own promotional Pins. It is not offered to other users and does not sell personal information.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Wallace Promo Posts, an application operated by Wallace Briggs. The app supports the creation, scheduling, publishing, and management of book and music promotional content on the operator’s own Pinterest boards.

This policy does not replace Pinterest’s own privacy policy or terms. Pinterest independently controls the information it collects and processes through its service.

2. Information the app may access

When the operator authorizes the app through Pinterest, Wallace Promo Posts may access only the Pinterest information and permissions needed for its stated purpose, including:

  • the authenticated Pinterest account’s basic account information;
  • the operator’s own Pinterest boards and Pins;
  • information needed to create, schedule, publish, update, or review the operator’s own Pins; and
  • authorization credentials required to maintain the Pinterest connection.

The app does not seek access to other Pinners’ private information and does not collect information from the general public through this privacy-policy page.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate with Pinterest and perform actions requested by the operator, such as reading his own boards, creating or scheduling Pins, publishing promotional content, and checking the result of those actions.

The information is not used for behavioural advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated marketing.

4. Sharing, sale, and disclosure

Wallace Briggs does not sell or rent personal information accessed through Wallace Promo Posts. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the Pinterest connection, comply with applicable law, protect legal rights, or investigate misuse.

5. Retention and security

Information and authorization credentials are retained only for as long as needed to operate the app or satisfy legal obligations. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Access and control

Because Wallace Promo Posts is a personal-use app, it does not create public user accounts. The authorized Pinterest connection can be revoked through Pinterest’s account settings. A request concerning information associated with the app may also be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children’s privacy

Wallace Promo Posts is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when an update is published.
